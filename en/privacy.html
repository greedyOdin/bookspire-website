Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This is a translation for convenience. The German version is the binding one.

This policy explains what data the Bookspire app and this website process, why, and for how long. In short: without an account, Bookspire works entirely on your device. Only if you create an account are your books and your tower stored on a server, so you can use them on several devices and with friends. There are no ads, no tracking, and nothing is passed on for advertising.

2. This website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, date and time and browser details, to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and available website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools and loads nothing from other servers; even the fonts are served from this site. Only if you join the waitlist does it send your address to our server.

Waitlist

If you join the waitlist on the home page, we store your email address, the language of the page and the time, to let you know once when Bookspire is available in the App Store and on Google Play. For this the page sends your address to our server at Supabase (see section 4). You first receive an email with a confirmation link via Mailjet; only after confirming are you on the list. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time via the unsubscribe link or by email. We delete unconfirmed entries after 7 days, confirmed ones as soon as you unsubscribe or we no longer need the waitlist. If you come via a link from our social media profiles, we also note the platform with your entry (such as “instagram”), so we can see which profiles reach people; the legal basis for this is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The note is deleted with the entry.

As a thank-you, everyone on the waitlist gets the “First Page” set in the app. If you create an account in the app with the same address, our server checks whether it is confirmed on the waitlist; the app only learns yes or no. With the launch email you also receive a code that gets you the set with a different address or without an account. So that each entry brings the set only once, we note on the entry when it was redeemed and, if you were signed in, for which account; the note is deleted with the entry, and the link to the account also when you delete your account. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in giving you this thank-you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3. The app without an account

Without an account your data stays on your device: books, reading progress, ratings, notes, the furnishing of your reading room, your avatar and your dragon. None of it is sent to us. If you delete the app, this data is gone; a backup you export yourself stays wherever you save it.

Book search, covers and blurbs

When you search for a book, scan an ISBN, or the app fetches a missing cover or blurb, your device asks these services directly:

  • German National Library (Deutsche Nationalbibliothek), Deutscher Platz 1, 04103 Leipzig, Germany
  • Open Library, a project of the Internet Archive, San Francisco, USA

What is sent is the search term, the ISBN or title and author, and – for technical reasons – your IP address. These requests are needed for the search to work (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Each provider is responsible for its further processing.

If neither knows a book, the app asks Google Books (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) through our server. Your device sends the search term, the ISBN or title and author to our server at Supabase in Ireland (see “Where the data is stored” in section 4), which passes them on to Google without your IP address. Only the cover images of these results are loaded by your device directly from Google, which sends your IP address to Google. Our server does not store these requests; Supabase keeps short-lived logs of calls to find errors. This applies with or without an account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

The app also sends the search term or the ISBN to our server at Supabase in Ireland. The Bookspire catalog is there, with books readers entered themselves (see “The Bookspire catalog” in section 4). If a search finds nothing that fits, for example because of a typo, a list of known book titles there, built from the freely available Open Library data, also suggests the title you meant. Our server does not store the search term; Supabase keeps short-lived logs of calls to find errors. This applies with or without an account (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

If you cannot find a book, you can tell us what you are missing below the search results. Only when you tap “Send” do your search term, an optional note and the app's language and version go to our server at Supabase in Ireland. We do not store your name, account or device with it, not even with an account. We use this only to improve the book search and delete it after 180 days (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Camera

The camera is only used to scan a book's barcode (ISBN), and only if you allow it. No images are stored or sent.

Reminders

Reading reminders are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server for them.

App updates

The app downloads updates through the Expo service (650 Industries, Inc., Palo Alto, USA). Your IP address, the app version and your operating system are sent in the process. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in delivering fixes quickly (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. The app with an account

An account is optional. If you create one, we process the following data to sync your devices and offer the features with friends (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR):

  • your email address, to send you sign-in codes
  • username and display name
  • your library: book details, reading status and dates, current page, ratings, notes, favourites, and who you lent a book to
  • the furnishing of your reading room
  • how many pages you read on which day (for the monthly ranking)
  • which of your devices is currently being synced: a random ID for the device, whether it is an iPhone, iPad, Android device or browser, and when it was last synced
  • friendships, recommendations to friends, blocks and reports
  • which books you contributed to the Bookspire catalog (see below)
  • whether and until when you are a member of the Bookspire Guild, and which guild code you redeemed

What friends see

Friends only see what you share. In your profile settings you decide whether they may see your library, your tower, your activity and your friends list. If you are in the Bookspire Guild, your friends see that unless you turn it off; they do not see how long your membership runs or how you joined. Nobody but you sees your email address.

The Bookspire catalog

If the search cannot find a book and you enter it yourself with an account, you can make it findable for others. The switch for this is in the form and is on by default. Title, subtitle, authors, series and volume, publisher, year, page count, language and ISBN then go into the Bookspire catalog, which everyone finds in the search. Your name, reading status, rating, notes, dates and cover do not. Separately, we store that your account contributed the book, to limit contributions per day and prevent abuse; nobody but us can see this (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If you report an entry as wrong or inappropriate, we handle it like any other report. If you delete your account, the link to your account is removed; the book details stay in the catalog, as they say nothing about you.

Where the data is stored

Accounts and data are stored with Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd., Singapore). The servers are in Ireland (region eu-west-1), so within the EU. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is part of Supabase's terms of service; any access from outside the EU is covered by the EU Commission's standard contractual clauses it contains.

Sign-in emails are sent by Mailjet (Sinch Mailjet SAS, 13-13 bis, rue de l'Aubrac, 75012 Paris, France) as our processor. Through Mailjet we also receive reports you make about other people or catalog entries as an email, so we can review them quickly.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile. Your profile, library, reading room, reading days, friendships, recommendations, your guild membership and the link to your contributions to the Bookspire catalog are then removed from the server immediately. The data on your device stays until you delete it there. Without the app, you can ask by email, see Delete your account.

5. Error reports

If the app crashes or an error occurs, it sends a short report to our server: the error message, where in the code it happened, the app version and the operating system, and if you are signed in, your account ID. Books, notes or names are never included. The reports serve only to find and fix errors (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and are deleted after 90 days at the latest.

6. Our social media profiles

Bookspire has profiles on TikTok, Instagram, Threads, Pinterest and YouTube (each @bookspireapp). When you visit one of them, the platform processes your data under its own terms, often even if you have no account there. We have no influence on that. This website does not embed any content from these platforms; it only links to the profiles.

If you comment, tag a post or send us a message, we see your profile name and what you wrote. We use this to reply and to look after the profiles (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and store none of it outside the platform.

The platforms also show us statistics on how our profiles and posts are used, such as views, countries or age groups. These figures are aggregated; we cannot identify individuals in them. For the processing that produces these statistics, we are jointly responsible with the respective platform (Art. 26 GDPR). We use them to see which posts reach people (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can exercise your rights under section 7 with us or directly with the platform; we forward requests that only the platform can fulfil. The platforms also process data outside the EU, for example in the USA. More in their privacy policies:

  • TikTok: TikTok Technology Limited, 10 Earlsfort Terrace, Dublin 2, D02 T380, Ireland – privacy policy
  • Instagram and Threads: Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, D04 X2K5, Ireland – privacy policy, Threads supplement
  • Pinterest: Pinterest Europe Limited, Palmerston House, 2nd Floor, Fenian Street, Dublin 2, Ireland – privacy policy
  • YouTube: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland – privacy policy

7. Your rights

You have the right to

  • access the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification (Art. 16 GDPR) and erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR) – the app can export your library,
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR).

Just write to kontakt@bookspire.de. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live.

8. Changes

If Bookspire gains features that involve other data, we update this policy. The version published here applies.
