Delete your account

In the app

Open Profile in Bookspire and, in the Account & privacy card, tap Delete account. After you confirm twice, your account is deleted immediately. If you subscribed to the guild, the app tells you first: the subscription keeps running in the App Store or on Google Play until you cancel it there.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, send an email to kontakt@bookspire.de – ideally from the address you sign in to Bookspire with, and with your username if you know it. We delete your account within 30 days and confirm it by email.

What is deleted

Deleted from the server immediately:

  • your account with email address, username and display name,
  • your library with notes, quotes, ratings, moods, reading data and wishlist,
  • your reading room, your reading days, reading times and the monthly ranking,
  • friendships, recommendations, blocks and your reports,
  • your book clubs with your votes and your progress in them,
  • your guild membership,
  • your devices and their notification IDs.

On the device where you delete your account, the app also removes your books and your room. If you want to keep them, make a backup first under Data in your profile. On your other devices, what is stored there stays until you delete the app there.

What stays

  • Books you shared with the Bookspire catalog stay there without any link to you. They only contain book details such as title, author and ISBN.
  • Notes about books the search could not find are stored without an account and deleted after 180 days.
  • Error reports contain no content and are deleted after 90 days.
  • If you had a subscription, the purchase history for your account ID stays with RevenueCat, the service behind our subscriptions, and with the store. We delete it at RevenueCat on request; just send us an email.

Deleting individual data

You do not have to delete your whole account to get rid of individual data. In the app you can delete a book (on its page under Edit), a quote, a note, a friendship or your membership in a book club at any time. With an account, it then disappears from the server and your other devices too. You can turn notifications off in the app or in your device's settings. If you want anything else deleted, write to kontakt@bookspire.de; we take care of it within 30 days.
