1. Who may create an account

You may create an account if you are at least 16 years old. One account per person; the name you give must not pass you off as someone else.

2. What Bookspire offers

Bookspire helps you log your books and your reading progress and rewards you with a reading room that grows. With an account, your data is synced between your devices, and you can find friends, recommend books to them and visit their towers.

Bookspire is currently free. If paid features are added later, we will announce them in the app beforehand; purchases are then made through the App Store or Google Play under their terms. Nothing you have used for free until then will be charged retroactively.

3. Treating each other with respect

On Bookspire, friends see your name, your books, notes and recommendations as far as you share them. For everything you show there: we do not tolerate objectionable content or abusive behaviour. In particular, the following is not allowed:

  • insults, harassment, threats and hate against people or groups,
  • sexual, violent or otherwise objectionable content,

  • anything that breaks the law.

4. Reporting and blocking

If you see something that breaks these rules, you can report and block the person in the app. Blocked people no longer see anything from you and can no longer send you anything. We usually review reports within 24 hours. If content breaks these rules, we remove it and may suspend or delete the account of the person who posted it. We will let you know if we change any of your content because of a report, and you can object at kontakt@bookspire.de.
