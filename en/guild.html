Bookspire Guild

For everyone who wants to live in their tower.

Your tower grows with every book you read. The guild is for those who would rather live in it than just drop by: who want to make every floor a room of its own, turn the pages they read into special pieces and finally find out how they really read.

The guild opens when Bookspire launches on the App Store and Google Play.

First things first, so it doesn’t get lost in the small print: Bookspire stays free. Your library, every page you log, your statistics, your friends and a tower that grows with every book belong to everyone, and the guild changes nothing about that. It takes nothing away. It adds something on top.

Every floor a room of its own.

Without the guild you furnish the ground floor however you like, and above it your tower keeps growing, floor by floor. You can step into the upper floors and look at your books there, but they are asleep: shelves, a window, a plain set of basic furniture.

As a guild member you wake them up, and every floor gets a character of its own. Perhaps walls of starstone and a red armchair beside the shelf on the first floor, mossy stone, a light birch floor and candles by the window on the second. Everything your books unlock can go on any floor. A tall tower becomes a house of many rooms, and each one tells of a different stretch of your reading.

Reading coins are earned by reading.

For every 250 pages you log in a month, Bookspire drops a reading coin into your purse, two at most per month. The purse holds twelve, and everyone can collect them, with the guild or without. Coins can’t be bought. Whoever has one has read for it.

In the guild shop you trade each coin for a piece of your choice. A desk under a toadstool cap is waiting there, and a hanging chair to disappear into with a book, a gramophone, a suit of armour keeping watch over your shelves and a little orange tree in its tub. Your avatar can have a cup of tea, a magic wand, headphones or a long knitted scarf. Each piece carries the guild’s little golden tower somewhere, as a fitting, a bit of embroidery or a print.

Two new pieces arrive every month, and none ever leaves, so there is nothing you could miss. Whatever you have traded for is yours for good, even if you leave the guild one day.

A closer look at your reading.

The statistics for everyone count your books and pages. Guild statistics ask further: how many days does a book take you on average, and which one did you devour the fastest? On which weekdays do you read the most, and how long was your longest run of reading days?

They show you how long your books are, which genre you rate best, which series you read from and in which languages. They set your year beside the last one and even tell you at which page you tend to put a book down when it doesn’t hold you. And they gather your highlights: the longest book, your most-read authors and your favourite genres.

And what else comes with it

A banner for your tower

The wine-red guild banner flies from the roof of your tower, and two more hang beside the door. Your profile shows your initial in gold, and when your friends visit, they see that you belong to the guild. If that’s more attention than you’d like, simply switch it off in your profile.

All your devices

Without the guild, one device is synced. As a member you log pages on your phone at lunch and on your tablet in the evening, and both know the same story, because your library and your tower are synced on all your devices at once.

What we promise you

What you have stays

If your membership ends, the upper floors rest exactly as you furnished them. Nothing is cleared away, your guild pieces stay yours, and when you come back, everything is there again at once.

Progress only by reading

Floors, unlocks and reading coins come from books and pages, never from money. The guild gives you more to do, but no head start.

No pressure

There is no countdown, no piece that is only around for a few days, and no offer that pops up when you open the app.

Your data is never held hostage

Library, statistics and export stay free for everyone. You can take your books with you at any time, whether you are in the guild or not.

Questions about the guild

What does the guild cost?

We’ll announce the price at launch. There will be a monthly subscription and a cheaper yearly one, and before you decide, you can try the guild for free for a while.

When can I join?

As soon as Bookspire is on the App Store and Google Play. If you join the waitlist, we’ll write to you when it’s time, and at launch you’ll get the set “First Page”.

What happens to my reading coins while I’m not in the guild?

They still collect in your purse until it’s full and wait there for you. You can already look around the guild shop, and if you join later, you can trade them right away.

What happens if I leave the guild?

Your upper floors then rest just as you furnished them, and you keep furnishing the ground floor however you like. Your guild pieces stay yours and can be placed on the ground floor. You keep collecting reading coins, the guild statistics turn back into a preview, and one device is synced. If you join again, everything is back at once.

How do I cancel?

At any time in the subscription settings of the App Store or Google Play. Your membership then runs until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Do imported books count?

For your tower, yes: every book you’ve read fills the shelves, even if it comes from Goodreads or another app. Reading coins, however, only come from pages you log in Bookspire from the guild’s launch on, because they are meant to show what you are reading now.

Do I need an account?

For the guild, yes, so that your membership counts on all your devices. Bookspire itself works without an account.

Will my friends see that I’m in the guild?

Yes. A small mark appears next to your name in their list, and when they visit your tower, it says “In the guild”. If you’d rather not, switch it off in your profile under “Account & privacy”.
