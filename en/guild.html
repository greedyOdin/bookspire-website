Bookspire Guild

The whole tower is yours.

As a guild member you furnish every floor of your tower, trade your reading coins for special pieces and learn from the guild statistics how you really read.

The guild opens when Bookspire launches on the App Store and Google Play.

Every floor a room of its own.

Above the ground floor, the floors of your tower are asleep until you wake them as a guild member. Then each one gets a character of its own: starstone and a red armchair on the first floor perhaps, mossy stone and a light birch floor above. Your tower becomes a house of many rooms.

Reading coins are earned by reading.

In the guild shop you trade each reading coin for a piece of your choice, such as a hanging chair to disappear into with a book, a gramophone or a cup of tea for your avatar. Two new pieces arrive every month, none ever leaves, and whatever you have traded for is yours for good.

You earn the coins for every 250 pages you log in a month, two at most per month. They can’t be bought.

A closer look at your reading.

Guild statistics tell you how many days a book takes you, on which weekdays you read the most, which genre you rate best and at which page you tend to put a book down when it doesn’t hold you. And they set your year beside the last one.

And what else comes with it

A banner for your tower

The wine-red guild banner flies from the roof of your tower, two more hang beside the door, and your profile shows your initial in gold. Whether your friends see it is up to you.

All your devices

You log pages on your phone at lunch and on your tablet in the evening, and both stay in step, because as a member all your devices are synced at once.

What we promise you

What you have stays

If your membership ends, the upper floors rest just as you furnished them. Your guild pieces stay yours.

Progress only by reading

Floors, unlocks and reading coins come from books and pages, never from money.

No pressure

There is no countdown, no piece that is only around for a while, and no offer that pops up when you open the app.

Your data is never held hostage

Library, statistics and export stay free for everyone. You can take your books with you at any time.

Questions about the guild

What does the guild cost?

We’ll announce the price at launch. There will be a monthly and a cheaper yearly subscription, and you can try the guild for free first.

When can I join?

When Bookspire launches on the App Store and Google Play. Join the waitlist and we’ll write to you as soon as it’s time.

What happens to my reading coins while I’m not in the guild?

They still collect in your purse and wait there. If you join later, you can trade them right away.

What happens if I leave the guild?

Your upper floors rest just as you furnished them, and your guild pieces stay yours. The guild statistics turn back into a preview, one device is synced, and you keep collecting reading coins. If you join again, everything is back at once.

How do I cancel?

At any time in the subscription settings of the App Store or Google Play. Your membership runs until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Do imported books count?

For your tower, yes: every book you’ve read fills the shelves. Reading coins only come from pages you log in Bookspire from the guild’s launch on.

Do I need an account?

For the guild, yes, so that your membership counts on all your devices. Bookspire itself works without an account.

Will my friends see that I’m in the guild?

Yes, by a small mark next to your name and by “In the guild” when they visit your tower. You can switch it off in your profile under “Account & privacy”.
