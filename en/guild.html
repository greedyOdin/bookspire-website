Bookspire Guild

For everyone who wants to live in their tower.

Bookspire is free and stays that way. Your library, every page you log and a tower that grows with your books belong to everyone. If you want to furnish every floor, trade reading coins for special pieces and take a closer look at your own reading, join the guild.

The guild opens when Bookspire launches on the App Store and Google Play.

What stays free, and what the guild adds

For everyone, for good

  • Your library with reading list, stars, notes and tags
  • Logging pages, a reading goal and statistics, plus the year in review to share
  • A tower that grows with every book you read, imported ones too. You can step into every floor and look at your books there
  • The ground floor is yours to furnish as you like, with everything your books unlock
  • Your avatar and the egg on the ground floor
  • Friends, recommendations, visits and the monthly ranking
  • Collecting reading coins and browsing the guild shop
  • One device is synced, and everything comes back when you switch phones
  • Import and export as a CSV file, backups

With the guild

  • Furnishing every floor of your tower, not just the ground floor
  • Trading reading coins for special pieces in the guild shop
  • Guild statistics: how fast you read and on which days, how long your books are, your year compared with the one before, and a good deal more
  • Syncing all your devices at once, your phone and tablet for example
  • The guild banner on your tower and the golden initial in your profile

Every floor a room of its own.

Your tower grows for everyone, book by book. Without the guild the upper floors are asleep: you can step in and look at your books, but they aren’t furnished. As a guild member you give every floor its own character, with different walls, different armchairs and a different light.

Reading coins are earned by reading.

For every 250 pages you log in a month you earn a reading coin, two at most per month. Your purse holds twelve. Everyone can collect them, with or without the guild. They can’t be bought.

In the guild shop you pick a piece for each coin: a toadstool desk, a gramophone, a cup of tea for your avatar. Each one carries the guild’s little golden tower. Two new pieces arrive every month, and none ever leaves.

A closer look at your reading.

Guild statistics pick up where the statistics for everyone stop: how many days a book takes you, on which weekdays you read the most, how long your books are, which genre you rate best and how your year compares with the last.

What we promise you

What you have stays

If your membership ends, the upper floors rest exactly as you furnished them. Your guild pieces are yours for good.

Progress only by reading

Floors, unlocks and reading coins come from books and pages, never from money.

No pressure

No countdown, no pieces you can miss, and no offer that pops up when you open the app.

Your data is never held hostage

Library, statistics and export stay free. You can take your books with you at any time, guild or not.

Questions about the guild

What does the guild cost?

We’ll announce the price at launch. There will be a monthly and a yearly subscription, and you can try the guild for free first.

When can I join?

When Bookspire launches on the App Store and Google Play. Join the waitlist and we’ll write to you as soon as it’s time.

What happens if I leave the guild?

Your upper floors rest just as you furnished them, and you keep furnishing the ground floor. Your guild pieces stay yours, you keep collecting reading coins, and the guild statistics turn back into a preview. One device is synced then. If you join again, everything is back at once.

How do I cancel?

At any time in the subscription settings of the App Store or Google Play. Your membership runs until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Do imported books count?

For your tower, yes: every book you’ve read fills the shelves, including those from Goodreads and the like. Reading coins, however, only come from pages you log in Bookspire from the guild’s launch on.

Do I need an account?

For the guild, yes, so that your membership counts on all your devices. Bookspire itself works without an account.

Will my friends see that I’m in the guild?

Yes, by a small mark next to your name and by “In the guild” when they visit your tower. If you’d rather not, switch it off in your profile under “Account & privacy”.
